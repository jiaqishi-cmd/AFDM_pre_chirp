# Communication Feasibility Gate

Turn broad research ideas into pursue, narrow, postpone, or discard decisions.

## Decision Axes

Evaluate:

- problem importance
- novelty source
- implementation path
- baseline availability
- experiment cost
- required theory
- data or simulation availability
- metric-to-claim alignment
- advisor and thesis fit

## Output

Return one decision:

- `PURSUE`: feasible and meaningful now.
- `NARROW`: promising but too broad; propose a smaller version.
- `POSTPONE`: interesting but blocked by missing prerequisites.
- `DISCARD`: weak novelty, unfair assumptions, or poor thesis fit.

For every decision, include:

- main reason
- biggest risk
- smallest next experiment or reading task
- what evidence would change the decision

Be direct. The goal is to protect research time.

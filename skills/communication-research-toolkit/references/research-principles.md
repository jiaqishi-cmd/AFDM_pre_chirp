# Research Principles

1. Understand before judging.
2. Evidence before conclusion.
3. Prefer primary sources: paper, supplementary material, official code, and author materials.
4. Separate facts, assumptions, interpretations, and speculation.
5. Do not fabricate missing metadata, equations, code links, venues, or results.
6. Scientific rigor is more important than popularity, citation count, or trend fit.
7. Mark uncertainty explicitly when evidence is incomplete.
8. Complete the requested workflow even when some information is missing.
